Spectra at NYU

Spectra at NYU is the Courant chapter of Spectra, the association for LGBTQ+ mathematicians. We are a community of students, postdocs, and faculty who believe that mathematics is for everyone.

Join our mailing list!

About

Spectra is the national association for LGBTQ+ mathematicians. Our NYU chapter brings that mission to Courant: to make the department a place where queer and trans mathematicians are visible, supported, and celebrated at every stage of their careers.

We host talks, socials, and reading groups. We pair undergrads with grad mentors. And we nudge the department on the boring but important stuff, like inclusive policies and making sure new students know they have people here.

  • ∀

    Open to all

    Undergrads, grad students, postdocs, faculty, and allies from any department. No membership required.

  • ∪

    Community first

    Mathematics is a social activity. We make space to be fully yourself while doing it.

  • ∂

    Change at the boundary

    We advocate for practical improvements in the department, one small step at a time.

What we do

∫

Mentorship

Undergraduates paired with grad students and postdocs. Ask about research, grad school, or how to survive analysis.

≅

Reading group

A member-chosen topic each semester. Past groups: symplectic geometry, category theory, the history of computation.

g ∘ f

Socials

Board games, picnics in Washington Square, the occasional museum. Sometimes there is math. Often there is pizza.

∂

Advocacy

Working with Courant on gender-inclusive facilities, name and pronoun policies, and climate surveys.
